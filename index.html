Bach Polyphonic Transformer

A multi-track generative model for Bach Chorales using the MMM representation. Adapts the Transformer (GPT-2) architecture to compose 4-part SATB harmony through track-concatenated symbolic sequences, with support for note-density conditioning and sequential voice generation.

Exploration of vocal technique transfer in EnCodec latent space, using the GTSinger dataset. Investigates whether latent interpolation between paired Control and Technique recordings can convincingly transfer singing styles such as Vibrato, Breathy, and Glissando.
